Introduction ​

AcesMotion is an animation plugin for Roblox Studio plus a small runtime that games install. Its animations adapt to the game they play in. A scene is cast by role, so it plays on whichever characters your game passes in. Motion Warp bends a punch, a throw or a projectile so it lands where the target really is, and constraints, drivers and generators react to the live scene while it plays.

A scene holds any number of actors (rigs, models, parts, cameras, lights, particle emitters, any instance) animated together on one Timeline. Your game plays it straight from its data, with no animation upload.

What you can do ​

  • Make an attack land on its real target with Motion Warp: you animate the trip once, and the game fits it to where things really are.
  • Cast actors by role, so one scene plays on any player, NPC or tagged object.
  • Aim, plant and limit with constraints (Track To, Damped Track, Lock, Floor, Limit Rotation and more), and set values from the live scene with drivers (Distance, Speed, Copy, Property).
  • Add shakes, wiggles, flickers and recoils without keys using generators.
  • Key any property of any instance from the Inspector: colours, sequences, gradients, numbers, text and attributes.
  • Preview particle bursts while you scrub, along with sounds, their waveforms, trims and fades.
  • Animate cameras: pose camera actors, look through them, switch shots with a Camera track, and key screen effects (letterbox bars, vignette, blur, fades, colour correction, bloom) that the game draws too.
  • Pose joints in the viewport with pins, context IK, joint limits, mirror posing and onion skin, then set timing on a dope sheet and shape curves (Channels and Ease views, saved presets).
  • Play the scene in a game with the runtime, in sync for every player, or export a scene that fits a Roblox animation as a native KeyframeSequence or CurveAnimation.

Who it's for ​

  • Combat and ability developers can build skills whose grabs and projectiles land on the real target, with VFX and a hit frame your server code can trust.
  • Cutscene makers can animate a whole cast, cut between cameras and add sound, with playback in sync for every player.
  • Animators coming from Blender or Maya find the Bezier curves, tangents, onion skin, motion trails, pins and IK they already use.
  • Beginners get a plugin that looks like Studio, where every button has a tooltip and a disabled button tells you why.
