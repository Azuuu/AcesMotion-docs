Generators ​

A generator drives a value over a span of time on top of its keys. A camera keyed from A to B can shake while it travels, a light can flicker, a prop can spin, a colour can cycle. You set the generator's settings instead of keying every wobble, and the same Seed plays the same in Studio, in exports and in games.

Adding one ​

Right click an empty spot on a track's lane, or a channel's, and choose Add generator. A Generators lane appears right under that row, holding the new block. The block spans the keys selected on that row, or one second from where you clicked when none are. Add generator is also in a row header's menu (an actor row adds it on its root) and in the Inspector's property row menu.

A camera actor's row menu has Add camera shake: a Shake on its root with the Handheld preset. The Library's Generators tab adds presets to the selected rows, or onto a row you drag them to.

The built-in generators ​

GeneratorWhat it does
Shake, WiggleSeeded noise; Shake can die away with Decay
OscillateA regular wave
Flicker / Strobe / PulseSwitches on/off values such as Enabled, or flickers a number
SpinTurns continuously
RecoilA kick and a bounce back, played once
Overshoot / SettleA spring that follows the value under it, overshooting each change and settling
SteppedHolds the value for 2, 3 or 4 frames at a time, for an on-twos look
Hold / FadeHolds a number at its Value, easing in and out by the block's fades
Cycle / FlipbookSteps through a list of values: enums, image ids, text
TypewriterTypes out a text, keeping rich text tags whole

Generators drive numbers, vectors, UDim and UDim2 (a GUI's Position or Size), colours, NumberRanges, sequences, an actor's root and joints, and on/off values. One that can't drive a row is greyed in the menu with the reason.

Blocks on the Timeline ​

Blocks draw in magenta with the generator's icon, name and preset ("Shake · Handheld"). Drag to move (Ctrl copies), drag an edge to trim, and drag a top corner grip to fade in or out. Overlapping blocks stack in sub-lanes; lanes apply in order, sub-lanes top to bottom.

Right click a block for Blend, the fade shapes, Fit to playback range, Reseed, Save as preset..., Bake to keys (writes the result as a key every frame and removes the block), Duplicate and Delete.

Click a Generators lane's arrow to open its settings rows: one row per number setting and Influence, holding that setting's keys. They select, drag and nudge like any keys.

The generator panel ​

Select a block and the Inspector shows its panel:

SectionHolds
PreviewThe block across its length with the playhead. Shape shows the generator alone, Result shows it applied on the keys
TimingStart, Length, and Snap to Loop
TargetWhat it drives, which axes or channels, and Space (Local or World)
SettingsAmplitude, Frequency, Decay, Period and the rest, Seed with Reseed, and Vary each play
BlendMode, Influence, Fade in and Fade out with their shapes
PresetThe generator's presets, and Save preset

Each setting has a key diamond, so a shake can build up or calm down over the block.

Dragging the preview ​

The preview is a control too. Drag up or down to change the size (Amplitude, Depth, Speed), left or right for the timing (Period, Frequency, Rate, Characters), or near a top corner for a fade. A click moves the playhead, and the wheel over a Shake or Wiggle steps its Seed. Shift+drag changes a second setting: Oscillate's Phase, a Strobe's Duty, a Shake's Decay, a Recoil's Kick and Bounce, a Typewriter's Delay. The preview's tooltip lists the drags for the generator you're on.

The scale is set when a block first shows and never refits, so a bigger Amplitude clips at the edges and a smaller one draws a smaller wave. Pin keeps the preview at the top while you scroll, and the window button opens a larger Generator window that follows the selected block.

Drive modes ​

Some value types can be driven in more than one way. Drive in the Target section picks it:

ValueModes beyond Around the value
ColourAlong a gradient (edited in the gradient editor) or HSV channels
NumberBetween Low and High
PositionOrbit in a plane, as a circle or a figure 8
On/offPattern, a grid of 1 to 32 steps you click
NumberRangeMove, Spread, Between two ranges
SequenceMove, Morph between two, Scroll

Looping ​

Snap to Loop (in Timing) makes a Shake, Wiggle, Oscillate, Flicker, Spin or Cycle loop seamlessly. Its speed snaps to the nearest value where whole repeats fit the block: type a 1.1 s Period on a 3 s block and it becomes 1 s, three waves. The field always shows what plays. Right click the block > Fit to playback range to put it over the loop region, or the whole scene.

Tips ​

  • Clamp keeps colour channels between 0 and 1. Turn it off for lights and Highlights that take brighter than white, and leave it on for a Part's Color, which wraps round past 1.
  • A hue cycle on a grey or white shows nothing. Type S value 1 (and V value 1) to see it at full colour.
  • Custom generators are ModuleScripts tagged AcesMotionGenerator. New custom generator... in the Add generator menu makes a typed, commented template. Custom generators walks through writing one.
  • Keep a custom generator's Offset pure and use the noise it's given for randomness, so every player sees the same thing.

Compared to Roblox's Animation Editor ​

Roblox's Animation Editor has no procedural generators: a shake or a flicker there is keyed by hand, frame by frame, and only on a rig's joints. In AcesMotion a generator rides on top of the keys of any property, root or joint, and Bake to keys turns it into ordinary keys when you want to edit it by hand.
