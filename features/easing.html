Easing and curves ​

An ease sets how a value travels from one key to the next: slow at first, fast at the end, overshooting and settling back. AcesMotion gives you three places to set it. The Ease window floats beside your work and follows the selection, the Ease view sits in the Timeline, and the Library's Easing tab holds every preset. All three share the same presets, including the ones you save.

The Ease window ​

Select keys and press E. The key menu's Ease... and the Ease button in the track column header open it too. It opens only when the selected keys have a next key to ease toward; otherwise the status bar says why.

From the top, the window holds:

PartWhat it does
Ease headerThe range being eased, and a + that saves the curve the selection shares as a preset
CurveA canvas with two handles and the X1, Y1, X2, Y2 fields. Dragging a handle updates the curve, the fields and the scene as you go, and lands as one undo step when you let go
RecentThe last 8 presets you applied anywhere, newest first
Search and presetsEvery preset, your saved ones first. One click applies a card

The window stays open and follows your selection, and Studio remembers where it sits. The Curve section folds and stays folded if you only want presets. With no segment selected everything greys out under "Select keys to ease them." Press Esc, E again, or its close button to close it.

The Ease view ​

Press Tab in the Timeline to cycle Dope sheet, Ease view, Channels view. In Split layout the Ease tab under the search box opens it under the dope rows.

The Ease view draws one progress curve for everything selected:

  • A summary bar names what you're easing ("r6 · 6 channels · f0 to f15") with chips for the segment count and whether they share an ease (Bezier, Same ease, Custom ease or Mixed).
  • The canvas has two handles. X stays between 0 and 1, Shift snaps to 0.05 and Alt ignores snapping. Dragging a handle past 100% washes the overshoot zone.
  • The Handles column has the X1, Y1, X2, Y2 fields and a Preset dropdown.
  • Save as preset keeps the curve, and Reset puts the segments back to Cubic with Auto tangents.

It works on the selected keys' segments. With no keys selected, it eases the segment under the playhead on every channel of the selected rows. A drag previews live in the viewport and lands as one undo step ("Set ease on N channels"). Cubic segments shaped by tangents read Custom.

GroupPresets
BasicLinear, In and Out (soft, medium, strong), Back, Snap, Anticipate, Elastic, Bounce, Step

Each Principles preset's tooltip says when to use it.

The Library's Easing tab ​

The Library's Easing tab lists the same presets under Built in and Saved. Click a card to select it, then double click it or press Apply to selection to put it on the selected keys' segments. Save current curve keeps the ease the selection shares.

Under Saved, right click a preset to rename it or delete it. Export presets... and Import presets share them with your team as a file (see The Library).

Eases on single keys ​

The Key panel in the Inspector shows a key's Interpolation: Constant, Linear, Cubic, Eased or Bezier.

InterpolationExtra fields
CubicTangent mode, In slope and Out slope
EasedStyle and Direction
BezierPreset, a small ease preview you can drag, and X1 Y1 X2 Y2

A new Bezier key starts from Settings > Curves > Default ease preset. The curve toolbar in the Channels view also has 8 preset tiles (Linear, Sine in-out, Quad in, Cubic in, Back in-out, Elastic in, Bounce out, Step). Those bake their shape into the selected segments as Cubic keys, so you can edit the tangents afterwards.

Other things that ease ​

  • Motion Warp trips. Select a warp bar with no keys selected and press E. The Ease window re-times the trip along the same path.
  • Number sequences. While a sequence editor is open, applying a Library ease lays it on the selected segment as up to 8 new points.
  • Driver mapping. A driver's Shape uses the ease presets to curve its mapping.

Tips ​

  • Deleting a saved preset asks first, because presets live in your plugin settings and Ctrl+Z can't bring them back.
  • The Recent list fills from every place you apply a preset, so your favourite eases end up one click away in the Ease window.

Compared to Roblox's Animation Editor ​

Roblox's Animation Editor eases a rig's keyframes with easing styles and directions and has its own curve editor. In AcesMotion the same eases work on every track the scene holds, so a camera's FieldOfView, a light's Brightness or a Motion Warp's trip can ease with the same Wind up or Bounce you use on a joint.
