Mouse gestures ​

Clicks, drags and modifiers in each window.

Timeline ​

Ruler and regions ​

GestureWhat it does
Drag the rulerScrubs. Snaps to markers, keys, regions, peaks, else whole frames; Alt flips snapping. Plays audio snippets over sounds.
Right click the rulerRuler menu (marker, loop, export region, insert/remove time).
Click a teammate's flagMoves your playhead to theirs.
Right click a teammate's flagThe teammate menu (Follow, Jump, Ping).
Middle drag or Space+drag the rulerPans time.
Drag a marker flag or stemMoves it (snaps).
Double click a marker flag or stemRenames it.
Right click a marker flag or stemMarker menu.
Drag the body of the loop bar or export bracketMoves the region.
Drag an end of the loop bar or export bracketResizes the region.
Drag the scene end handleChanges the scene length (snaps; Alt flips Snap).

Keys in the lanes ​

GestureWhat it does
Click a keySelects it.
Shift+click / Ctrl+click a keyAdds to / toggles in the selection.
Double click a main keySelects everything under it.
Drag a keyMoves the selected keys, with a chip and ghosts. Snaps; Alt flips Snap.
Ctrl+drag a keyMoves a copy.
Drag on an empty laneBox select; Shift / Ctrl adds.
Double click empty lane space on a rowSelects every key of that row (Shift / Ctrl adds).
Drag a scale handleRetimes the selection about the other end; Shift about the playhead.
Drag the spread handleOffsets joints' keys in time (Random / Alternate / Staircase). Drag left for earlier; Alt for exact ticks.
Click the spread chipSpread menu (mode, Reroll random).
Right click a keyKey menu.

Action lane ​

GestureWhat it does
Click / Shift+click / Ctrl+click a blockSelects it / adds it / toggles it (keys let go).
Drag a blockMoves the selected blocks in time (their own tracks), with ghosts and a dotted trail. Snaps; Alt flips Snap.
Ctrl+drag a blockMoves a copy.
Drag a sound bar, burst bar or block up or downMoves that bar to the lane under the pointer (with any time move, one undo step). Works in the lanes and the action lane.
Drag a bar's end gripTrims that end; the other stays (at least one frame). Shows a "Length 60 f" read-out.
Drag on an empty spotBox-selects blocks (into other rows: their keys and blocks).
Double click an empty spotSelects every block of the track.
Right click a blockBlock menu.
Right click an empty spotTrack menu (Add block at that frame).

Camera row ​

GestureWhat it does
Click / Shift+click / Ctrl+click a shotSelects its switch key / adds it / toggles it (keys and blocks let go).
Drag a shotMoves the selected switch keys in time. Taken frames are skipped; snaps, Alt flips Snap.
Ctrl+drag a shotMoves a copy.
Drag on an empty spotBox-selects switch keys (down into the rows: their keys and blocks instead).
Double click an empty spotSelects all switch keys.
Drag a shot's right endCuts the shot short (its Until; no camera live to the next key). Onto the next key, it runs to it again.
Right click a shotKey menu: Switch to, Start at, End at, Extend to the next switch or scene end, Duplicate, Delete, Show in Inspector.
Right click an empty spotLane menu: Switch to at that frame, Add camera at view.
Right click the headerHeader menu: Select all switch keys, Mute, Lock.

Sound and effect bars ​

GestureWhat it does
Drag a sound bar's left edgeTrims the start (the audio stays put).
Drag a sound bar's right edgeTrims the end.
Double click a sound bar's right edgeClears the end trim.
Ctrl+drag a sound bar's right edgeStretches the speed (0.25x to 4x).
Drag a sound bar's fade gripFades.
Drag a sound bar's loop handleMoves the loop.
Right click a sound fade gripFade menu (shape, remove).
Drag an effect bar's end gripMax life.
Drag an effect bar's min knobMin life.
Double click an effect bar's gripResets it to the emitter's own.

Rows ​

GestureWhat it does
Click / Ctrl+click / Shift+click / Ctrl+Shift+click a row headerSelect / toggle / range / add range.
Double click a row headerKeys the row at the playhead (like K).
Right click a row headerRow menu.
Drag the top or bottom edge of a sound or effect row headerTaller or shorter row. Double click resets.
Hover a row headerShows the mute (eye) and lock buttons.
Drag the splitter (Split)Shares the room between dope rows and curves.

Scrolling, zoom and pan ​

GestureWhat it does
Mouse wheel over the lanesScrolls rows.
Ctrl+wheel over the lanes, ruler or graphZooms time at the cursor.
Shift+wheel over the lanes, ruler or graphPans time.
Ctrl+Shift+wheel over the lanesCompact or normal rows.
Middle drag or Space+drag over the lanes, ruler or graphPans.

Curve graph and ease ​

GestureWhat it does
Click / Shift / Ctrl a keySelects / adds / toggles.
Drag selected keysMoves in time and value. Shift keeps one direction; Alt flips snapping.
Drag a tangent handleShapes both sides; Alt shapes one side.
Wheel / Ctrl+wheel / Shift+wheel / Ctrl+Shift+wheelPans values / zooms time / pans time / zooms values.
Wheel over the value axisZooms values at the pointer.
Drag a handle in the Ease canvasShapes the ease live. Shift snaps 0.05; Alt ignores snapping.
Click an Ease preset cardApplies it.
Right click a saved Ease preset cardDeletes it (after a confirm).

Status bar, toolbar and tooltips ​

GestureWhat it does
Click snap, auto-key, fps or length in the status barOpens the menu or toggles.
Click a tool's chevron in the toolbarThe tool's options menu.
Hover any controlTooltip (0.5 s delay, then instant across neighbours). Disabled controls say why.

Hub ​

GestureWhat it does
Click a scene rowOpens it.
Double click a scene rowRenames it in place.
Hover a scene rowRename, Duplicate, Delete buttons.
Click / double click / right click a cast rowSelects / renames / menu.
Click a cast row chipAlign menu.
Hover a cast rowMute (eye) and lock buttons.

Inspector ​

GestureWhat it does
Click a number fieldTypes a value.
Drag a number field left or rightScrubs. Shift finer (10x), Ctrl whole numbers, Esc reverts.
Right click anywhere on a property rowRow menu.
Click a key diamondAnimate / key / remove key / key a change.
Hover a property row + KKeys it.
Click a section headerFolds it (remembered per class).
Click a Color3 swatch or a sequence/gradient previewOpens the editor window.
Click a texture palette swatchSwaps the image.
Click a stack entryOpens its block's panel (the Attached entry: its attach key).
Right click a stack entryIts block menu.
Drag a stack entry up or downChanges the order it runs in; Esc puts it back.
Click a stack entry's eyeMutes or unmutes it.

Editor windows ​

GestureWhat it does
Drag the hue ring / squareSets hue / saturation and brightness.
Click the sequence graphAdds a point and keeps dragging it.
Drag a sequence pointMoves it (Shift snaps time).
Right click a sequence pointRemoves it.
Drag the envelope barWidens or narrows the spread.
Click the gradient barAdds a stop.
Drag / right click / double click a gradient stopMoves / removes / opens its colour picker.
Click / right click a saved swatchApplies / rename and delete menu.

Viewport ​

GestureWhat it does
Click a body part, joint dot or bonePicks it; Ctrl adds or removes.
Click empty spaceClears the pick and Studio's selection.
Click outside the sceneSelects the model in Studio; Ctrl toggles.
Alt+click outside the sceneSelects the part itself in Studio; Ctrl toggles.
Drag a gizmo handleTurns or moves the picked joints. Ctrl flips the snapping; Esc or right click cancels.
Drag a resize handleResizes. Ctrl both sides, Alt keeps proportions, Esc cancels.
Press and drag a part (IK on)IK drag of its chain.
Hold P + click a part / a joint dot / a floor or propPins a spot / the whole part / anchors the picked parts' pins.
Hold Tab over a partTemporary pivot.
X when not draggingSwitches FK/IK. Mid drag it locks the X axis.
Hold J over a trailTiming mode.
Click / drag a trail dot or handlePicks / reshapes.
Double click a trail lineAdds a key.
