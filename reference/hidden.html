Hidden things ​

Easy-to-miss controls and tricks.

Timeline ​

  • Space does two jobs: tap it to play or pause, hold it and drag to pan. The status bar says "Space held: drag to pan" while it is armed.
  • Alt means "no snapping" everywhere: scrubbing the ruler (lands between frames), key and marker drags, region and scene-end drags, scale handles, sound and effect bar edges, ease handles (Alt ignores the Shift snap). Spread uses Alt for exact ticks instead of whole frames.
  • Double click does a lot: a row header keys the row at the playhead; empty lane space selects all of that row's keys (on an action track, all its blocks); a main key selects everything under it; a marker flag renames it; a sound's right edge clears its trim; a bar grip resets to the emitter's own Lifetime; a row-height grip resets the row's height; a scene in the list renames it.
  • Tab is a three-stop cycle: Dope sheet, Ease view, Channels view, back to the dope sheet (not just two views). In Split it swaps the lower part between Ease and Channels.
  • The Ease and Channels tabs under the Search box open that view under the dope rows; clicking the open one closes it.
  • E opens the Ease window for the selected keys without going to the Ease view.
  • The value axis zooms: scroll over the 44 px axis in the curve graph to zoom values at the pointer (Ctrl+Shift+wheel does it anywhere on the graph).
  • Ctrl+Shift+wheel shrinks or grows dope sheet rows (compact rows are 20 px, normal 24).
  • Click the status bar's items: Snap opens the snap menu, Auto-key toggles, the frame rate opens its menu, the length opens "Type a length... / End at the playhead / End at the last key" (type 2s, 72f or a number in the ruler's units), the particle-cap message opens Settings > Effects.
  • Retiming scales: dragging the scale handles, or shortening the scene, never deletes keys. Insert/Remove time in the ruler menu shifts everything after a point; "Remove the loop region's time" cuts the loop region out.
  • Shift+K (or a double click on an actor's row header) keys a whole actor: every joint of a rig, even the ones not keyed yet, never its root (key that from the root row); the root of anything else.
  • Frame 0 is kept for you: the first key you write after frame 0 on a track also keys frame 0 with the value from before you animated it (Settings > Posing > Key frame 0 when it's missing; Auto-key chevron).
  • Call rows: K or a double click on an Emit() / Play() row header keys a call at the playhead copying the key before it (an Emit(20) stays 20). Two Play() keys with different settings dropped on one frame both stay (stacked bars).
  • Stacked bars can be re-arranged: drag a sound bar, burst bar or action block up or down to choose its lane; right click > Reset lane to let it stack on its own.
  • Sound bars: trim the start without moving the audio, stretch the speed with Ctrl+drag, fade with the hover grips, loop with the loop handles, and drag a key near a loud hit to snap onto the waveform's peak (amber snap line, "snap: Roar peak"). A sound row can be made taller (drag the header edge) for bigger waveforms. Scrubbing over a sound plays short snippets (Settings > Audio) that end where the playhead's frame ends.
  • Emitter bars: grips on an Emit() bar set that burst's particle life (the key keeps its own life); the grips on an emitter's stream band change the emitter's Lifetime property.
  • Remove many tracks at once: Ctrl or Shift click several object or property headers, right click one: "Remove N tracks from timeline".
  • Mute / lock work per actor (Hub or the Timeline row), per joint and per property track. Muted rows draw at 50% and are skipped in preview, playback and export.
  • Paste to another actor: select exactly one other actor and paste (or use the key menu's "Paste to another actor") to put copied keys on it; Paste mirrored swaps a joint's keys onto its partner with the flipped channels negated.
  • Row stripes make a row's name line up with its keys; strength is tunable (View > Row stripes, Settings > Timeline).
  • The Timeline and the Hub set Studio's selection aside while the pointer is over them, so a stray Delete cannot delete your rig (a Cast row click selects the rig, which shows in the Explorer once the pointer leaves the Hub). If a stand-in is deleted anyway, a banner offers Restore.

Viewport ​

  • T switches between Move & rotate and Resize; R cycles Local / Parent / World; Ctrl during a drag flips between Studio's snapping increments and free movement.
  • Tap P pins the picked parts; hold P and click pins a spot; hold P and click a floor or prop (with parts picked) anchors their pins to it so they ride along; Unanchor picked pins (the Pins menu, or the viewport's right-click Unanchor pin) lets them go of it, pinned where they are now; Shift+P clears.
  • Hold I to flip FK and IK for a drag; Ctrl+[ / Ctrl+] change the IK chain length (even mid-drag).
  • Hold Tab to turn about any point of any part (27 snap points per part box); release and the gizmo goes back to the joint.
  • Hold J (or click the stopwatch) to retime keys by sliding their dots along a motion trail; double click a trail to add a key; Alt on a tangent handle breaks it.
  • Click an effect model in the viewport (even an invisible, non-clickable holder part) to select it in Studio, then Add actor from selection. Alt+click selects just the part.
  • [ and ] walk up and down the picked joint's chain.
  • Esc or right click cancels any gizmo or resize drag and puts the pose back.
  • Poses are temporary until keyed: moving the playhead drops an unkeyed pose; Auto-key keys on release; K keys picked joints.
  • Onion ghosts, trails and pins all read the keys you have selected in the Timeline: select two or more keys to see the path and a ghost on each.

Inspector ​

  • Drag any number field to scrub it (Shift 10x finer, Ctrl whole numbers, Esc puts it back).
  • Right click anywhere on a row: Key at playhead, Remove from timeline, Copy, Paste, Reset to default, Reset to value before animating.
  • K over a row keys it. The diamond cycles animate, key, remove key.
  • Edits made in Studio's own Properties window to a property already on the Timeline are recorded (keyed with Auto-key on, a Changed pose with it off).
  • Search covers the whole actor, not just the picked object; matches elsewhere are listed under "Elsewhere on {actor}" with a Show arrow.
  • Select several objects (Timeline keys or rows, or the Explorer) and edit them together: Mixed values, per-part typing, one undo step.
  • A Decal id pasted into a texture field says "Not an image: a Decal": paste the image's id instead (a Decal's Texture holds it). The palette lists the track's images and the actor's.
  • A NumberSequence / ColorSequence row has a one-value control before its preview.
  • Attributes of any type animate like properties; the Calls section keys methods.
  • Clicking a Timeline property row, a key, or a part in the viewport changes what the Inspector shows.
  • An actor or its root row? Both show the actor's properties, but the Stack section lists every stacked row for the actor (its Timeline row or its own root picked) and only the root's stack when the Timeline's root row is the selected one.

Editor windows ​

  • Enter = OK, Esc = Cancel, and Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y step through the window's own changes (also the footer's Undo / Redo). Keep several windows open at once, up to 6 per kind.
  • Click the sequence graph to add a point and keep dragging it; double click a gradient stop to edit its colour in its own picker; Presets replace the shape; the Saved strip keeps colours, gradients and sequences in the scene for every property.
  • A saved sequence larger than the current Range grows the Range to fit.

Scenes, export and settings ​

  • Scenes can live in any folder: move a scene folder anywhere and it is still listed and saved there; Export's play line then starts with AcesMotion.RegisterFolder(...).
  • Scene > Save as... makes a copy and opens it with its name ready to type; Duplicate copies without opening.
  • Export > Preview id makes a test AnimationId for the session so the rig plays the exported clip without uploading; after the first upload paste the id so you can pick "Overwrite an existing asset..." next time and keep the same id in games.
  • Scene as text (Export) + Import > Scene text moves a whole scene between places.
  • Create N scenes / Save as model (Export > AcesMotion runtime) split a multi-rig scene into one scene per rig and pack them for other games.
  • Reset this group at the bottom of a Settings page restores that page's defaults (and its rebindable keys) after a confirm; it never touches your mirror rules or presets.
  • Gizmo size: click the value on the slider to type one. Icon style switches every icon live.
  • Every control has a tooltip; a disabled control's tooltip says why. After the first tooltip, tooltips appear instantly while you move across neighbouring controls.
