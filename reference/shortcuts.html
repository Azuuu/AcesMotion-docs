Keyboard shortcuts ​

Every default key. Change them in Settings > Shortcuts.

How keys work ​

  • Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y are Studio's undo and redo. Every AcesMotion edit is a step, poses included, and Edit > Undo names it.
  • Keys are ignored while a text box has focus.
  • On a Mac, Cmd works wherever these pages say Ctrl (snapping flips, multi-select, shortcuts).

Keys Studio keeps

Some keys never reach a plugin, so the defaults avoid them:

  • Delete. It does arrive in the Timeline, because the Timeline sets Studio's selection aside.
  • Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+D and Shift+X.
  • O, , and . (Studio's camera keys).
  • Any key you or another plugin bound in Studio's File > Customize Shortcuts. Those are global, and a plugin cannot read them.

That is why copy is Shift+C, paste and duplicate are Shift+V / Shift+D, the frame steps are the arrows, and loop in and out are Shift+I / Shift+O.

Rebinding ​

  • Every shortcut can be rebound in Settings > Shortcuts. Right click a row to clear it or reset it.
  • The hold keys also have a field on their own page:
    • Temporary pivot key, Pin key (Shift plus it clears pins), IK / FK switch key and Look through key, on the Posing page.
    • Timing key, on the Trajectory page.
  • A key another shortcut already uses in the same place is refused ("X is taken by {name}"). So are Studio's own Ctrl+C, V, X, D, Z and Y.
  • Reset this group puts them back.

Global keys through Studio

Every shortcut is also a Studio plugin action named "AcesMotion: {name}", with no default key there. Bind it in Studio's own shortcut editor (File > Customize Shortcuts); Settings > Shortcuts says so under its header, with a tooltip.

  • A key bound there works anywhere in Studio, even when no AcesMotion window has focus.
  • Studio runs it before AcesMotion sees it, so a plain letter or Space bound there may fire while you type. Pick one with Ctrl or Alt.
  • Plugin actions fire on press only. The hold keys (Space, P, I, Tab, J) fire their release when the plugin action is used.

What Del deletes:

  • With blocks selected, it deletes the blocks instead. Keys and blocks selected together are deleted together.
  • With no keys or blocks selected, it deletes the selected marker.
  • Else it removes the selected rows' tracks, as one undo step: property, object, joint and root rows as Remove from timeline, action tracks as Remove action track.
  • Actor rows stay; a note says to use their menu.
  • Keys on a locked track or actor are left and stay selected. The status bar names the lock ("root is locked: 12 keys stayed selected. Unlock it to delete them.").

Camera ​

Q, Shift+Q and Ctrl+Shift+Q work in the viewport and the Timeline. F and Ctrl+F work in the viewport.

KeyActionNotes
QLook through cameraToggles look-through. It always turns it off, also while looking away.
Shift+QAlign camera to viewMoves the selected camera actor (else the live one) to your view and keys it at the playhead.
Ctrl+Shift+QPilot cameraTurns Pilot camera on (looking through first if needed) or off.
FFocus camera on the pointerTurns the camera to look at what's under the pointer, keeping position and roll (Reset roll when focusing levels it).
Ctrl+FFocus camera and level itAs Focus camera on the pointer, and levels the roll to 0.
  • All five are ignored while a mouse button is held. For Q that is because Studio flies down with right mouse + Q.
  • Q is also ignored with Ctrl, Alt or Meta held. While Q is held, the view stays still (Studio would fly it down).
  • When Shift+Q or Ctrl+Shift+Q can't run (the view is locked on the camera, no camera), the reason shows in the status bar.
  • Focus works with a camera picked (Move or Rotate gizmo), a camera selected in the Timeline or Hub, or the piloted camera. With no camera picked it does nothing.
  • Focus is keyed with Auto-key, as one undo step. While looking through, both Focus keys work only with Pilot camera on.

Menus ​

KeyAction
EscCloses the menu.
Up / DownMove, skipping greyed items.
Return / EnterRun the highlighted item or open its submenu.
RightOpens a submenu.
LeftCloses a submenu.
Any other keyCloses the menu.

With the mouse:

  • A submenu opens on hover and stays open while you move into it, even across other items on the way. It closes a moment after you settle on another item.
  • In a narrow window, a submenu that can't fit beside its menu opens just below its item.
  • Tooltips of things under an open menu stay hidden.
  • Menu items whose name doesn't say what they do show a tooltip card while hovered (about 250 across the Timeline, viewport, Hub and Inspector menus).
