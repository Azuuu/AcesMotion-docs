Changelog ​

What changed in each release, newest first. The same list is in the plugin under Settings > About.

0.20.0 2026-10-30

New

  • **See your teammates while you animate**: In Team Create, everyone with AcesMotion sees each other. Avatars sit on the Timeline toolbar, each teammate gets a playhead and a name flag on the ruler in their own colour, and what they pick shows as rings on keys, stripes on rows and their joints in the viewport with a name tag. While someone types in or drags an Inspector field, that row shows their colour and avatar. Nothing extra is saved into the place.
  • **Follow a teammate**: Click a teammate's avatar to put your camera and playhead on theirs. When they play, you play with them, sounds and effects included, and a teammate in another scene opens that scene first. Move the camera, scrub, pick something or press Play and you take back control, with a Follow button to pick up again. A chip in the viewport tells you who is following you. Studio's own Team Create camera marker for the person you follow still shows; it can't be hidden yet.
  • **Edit the same scene at the same time**: Two people can change the same actor at once and both changes stay: yours is applied again on top of theirs in the same undo step. Keys a teammate is dragging show a lock, but you can still move them, and whoever lets go last wins. Ctrl+Z only undoes your own steps, and if it would wipe a teammate's change you get Put back to restore theirs. Changing the frame rate or restoring a version asks first and names who is in the scene.
  • **Settings for working together**: Settings > Collaboration, the View menu and every teammate menu turn teammates' playheads, selections and the viewing chip on or off, only on your screen. People show by their display name.
  • **A Scenes tab built for big places**: Scenes now remember who made them and who edited them last. The Hub's Scenes tab groups them by the folders under AcesMotionScenes (or by creator), with collapsible groups, search by name or person, chips for Mine, Recent and In this session, a Made by filter and sorting. Make folders, rename them, drag scenes between them and move scenes kept elsewhere into AcesMotionScenes. It stays quick with a thousand scenes or more, and the Timeline's Scene field opens a picker of the same list.
  • **Resize limbs and key their size**: The Resize tool now works on the limbs of Motor6D and AnimationConstraint rigs. A limb grows away from the joint it hangs from, and the joints inside it move out with it, so a longer upper arm carries the forearm and hand down at their own size. Hats, hair and welded parts on it move out too. Flip pose, mirrored paste and the pose library carry limb sizes. Only the AcesMotion runtime plays them, since a Roblox animation can't hold part sizes. Bones can't resize.
  • **See which posing tool is on**: A small pill at the viewport's bottom left names the posing tool, Move and rotate or Resize, and turns amber with the reason when nothing picked can resize. Click it to switch, or right-click it for the handle options. Hide it from its menu, the View menu or Settings > Posing.

Improved

  • **New defaults: Auto-key on, Onion skin and Trajectory off**: New installs start with Auto-key on, and with Onion skin and the motion trail off. Settings you changed yourself stay as they were.
  • **A redrawn scene icon**

0.19.2 2026-10-29

Fixed

  • **Moves played again and again stay culled off screen**: A new play counted as on screen until its first visibility check, up to a quarter second later, so an off-screen rig replaying an attack every third of a second still posed most frames. Each play now checks on its first frame: off screen it never poses, on screen it poses every frame as before.

0.19.1 2026-10-29

Fixed

  • **Rigs with foot Locks stop posing off screen**: A rig whose stack had a Lock or a Floor posed every frame in games, even off screen, so a crowd of units cost as much as if all were in view. Constraints aimed at the rig itself, or at a spot in the scene, now follow level of detail like the rest of the rig. Markers and Event blocks still fire on time. A driver, a custom constraint or one aimed at another role still keeps it posing every frame.

0.19.0 2026-10-29

New

  • **Read marker times without playing**: AcesMotion.GetMarkers, GetEvents, GetMarkerTime and GetLength tell your game when a scene's markers and Event blocks fire and how long it plays, on the server or a client, from a scene or a reference. A combat move can look up its Hit time before the swing starts instead of keeping a copy in an attribute.
  • **Time maps on the server clock**: Track:SetTimeMap, or the TimeMap play option, ties a track's time to server time with a few knots. The track speeds up or slows down to reach each one, so a hit lands exactly when the server says. Markers fire once as the time crosses them, and every client, late joiners too, shows the same frame.

0.18.2 2026-10-29

Fixed

  • **Arms no longer swing over the head in a fade**: When one scene fades into another, a joint set to Each angle on its own now moves angle by angle, the way its keys do. Going from a raised guard idle to a run used to swing the arm straight up for a few frames.

0.18.1 2026-10-29

New

  • **See where Shortest turn is off**: A warning icon on a joint's row says where its turn falls back to each angle on its own, and why. Click it, or open Between keys in the row, key or Inspector menus, to fix it: Align keys gives every angle a key where one has, and Unwind turns a spin into the short way.

Improved

  • **Keys store angles that suit their neighbours**: Posing, pasting and mirroring a pose pick the angles that fit the keys before and after it, so the curves stay smooth and the graph shows fewer flips. The Euler filter also straightens poses stored the other way round, not just whole turns.

Fixed

  • **No more swinging past key poses**: With Between keys set to Shortest turn, a joint that reaches an extreme and comes back (every run cycle) used to swing up to 65 degrees past the key pose. The turn is now flat at those keys and never overshoots, like Auto tangents on a curve. Linear and Flat keys don't bend at all, and Smooth keys keep the old free curve. Games get the same motion.
  • **Near-identical poses don't spin round**: Two keys only a few degrees apart could spin almost a full turn when their angles were stored a turn or a flip apart. They now turn the short way. A real spin, one angle past 180 degrees with the others nearly still, still spins.
  • **Flip pose and Reset work from the Timeline**: Shift+F and Shift+R only worked with the viewport focused. They now work from the Timeline, Hub and the Inspector too, and with keys on joint rows selected in the Timeline they flip or reset those keys' joints at the playhead, in one undo step.
  • **Paste keys mirrored moves locked parts**: Pasting keys mirrored onto a joint with a Lock changed nothing: the lock held the part where it was. The lock now follows, keyed where the mirrored pose puts the part. Pasting keys from an unlocked joint onto a locked one does the same.
  • **Joint keys paste only onto the same joint**: Keys copied from a joint pasted onto a rig that doesn't have it, or onto whatever joint row was selected. They now go only onto the joint of the same name (its partner for Paste keys mirrored). Joints the rig doesn't have are skipped and a toast names them; if none are there, nothing is pasted.
  • **Copy pose and Flip read the keyed pose**: On a locked or constrained joint, Copy pose, Flip pose and Save pose took the constraint's result instead of the joint's own pose, so Paste mirrored in the viewport and Paste keys mirrored in the Timeline gave different keys. Both now give the same.

0.18.0 2026-10-29

New

  • **Scene origin can follow a role**: Under Scene origin in the Hub's Cast tab, set Origin to Follows and pick the role whose part the scene rides in a game, and which part (HumanoidRootPart by default). AcesMotion.Load(clip, model) then plays a punch effect on whoever throws the punch, with no Anchor in your code. The viewport shows the scene riding that role's stand-in, so it plays where you see it.
  • **References can pick their own origin**: A reference's Origin field in the Inspector uses the scene's setting, plays where the scene was authored, or follows another role. An Anchor passed in code still wins over both.

Improved

  • **One Scene origin group**: Relative to is now Characters move from (Own root part, Scene origin, Mixed), and the origin's place is one Origin setting: Where authored or Follows. Spawned actor rows say what they follow, such as follows Unit > Torso.
  • **First anchor filled in for you**: When a scene with one character gets its first spawned actor key, the scene is anchored to that character in the same undo step, and a toast says so with a Clear button. Clearing it keeps it cleared. Existing scenes are left alone.
  • **More numbers on the seconds ruler**: In seconds, the ruler labels as densely as it does in frames: zoomed in on a 2 s scene it reads 0.1s, 0.2s, 0.3s instead of every half second. Labels drop trailing zeros, like 0.25s and 1s.
  • **Anchors work for everyone**: PlayForAll, server plays and late joiners use the same anchor. A client finds the role's part on its own side when the character streams in or the cast changes.

Fixed

  • **Part pickers in the Hub**: Socket, Parent to and the origin's Part picker in the Hub's Cast tab open their list again. Before, the list only ever drew in the Inspector.

0.17.0 2026-10-28

New

  • **References window**: A table of every reference in the place, grouped by name, scene or owner. Point many references at a scene at once, fix ones whose scene or cast is gone, rename a move everywhere, and add references to many models. Picking rows selects the references in Studio, and the other way round.
  • **References toolbar button**: A third toolbar button opens the References window. It is also in View > Windows, in the Hub's Scenes tab, in the reference panel, and on a shortcut you can bind.
  • **Re-reference clips from the Explorer**: Select references and one scene, then click Create reference to point them at that scene. Their names stay, so code that plays them by name keeps working. Cast entries for roles the new scene doesn't have are removed, and the toast says how many.
  • **References go into any instance**: Create reference puts one into each selected instance of any class, such as a folder or a part, not only models. A target that already has one with that name gets a numbered copy, like BasicAttack2.

Improved

  • **Clip counts in the Hub**: Each scene in the Scenes tab shows how many references play it; click the count to see them in the References window. When you delete a scene that references use, the question turns orange and says how many.
  • **Reference docs are easier to read**: The reference pages use tables, short headings and key labels instead of long lists.

0.16.0 2026-10-28

New

  • **References you clone into NPCs**: A reference is a small Configuration that points at a scene by its id. Clone it into each NPC or its template, the way you would an Animation, and play it with AcesMotion.Play(npc, "Punch"). Renaming or moving the scene doesn't break it.
  • **Cast each role on the reference**: A reference can name who plays each role, with an Instance attribute named after the role. A target inside the same model follows the model into every clone. Roles you leave empty play your code's cast, else the actor's fallback.
  • **Create reference button**: A second toolbar button makes a reference to the scene selected in the Explorer: one in each selected model, or one in ReplicatedStorage to drag where you need it. It's also in the Scene menu and on each scene in the Hub.
  • **Reference panel in the Inspector**: Select a reference to see its scene, role and cast, point it at another scene, or open its scene. An empty cast field says what plays the role instead, such as the fallback.

Improved

  • **Scenes found by id**: A reference finds its scene by id in the scene folders and anywhere a scene is tagged, so scenes can live outside AcesMotionScenes. If two scenes share an id, the runtime warns once.
  • **Plays don't depend on scene names**: Relayed client plays and late joiners find the scene by its id, so a renamed scene still plays for everyone.

0.15.4 2026-10-28

Improved

  • **Socket and Parent to use the part picker**: In the Inspector and the Cast tab, both fields get the same picker as Attached to: a searchable list of the cast's parts, a viewport pick, Use selection and right click Clear.
  • **Spawn and Camera sections scroll**: They scroll with the properties, so a long Spawn section no longer pushes the rest off screen. Only the object picker and Search properties stay at the top.
  • **Studio shortcut bindings explained**: Settings > Shortcuts says that every command is also in Studio's Customize Shortcuts, and that a key bound there works even while you type, so pick one with Ctrl or Alt.

Fixed

  • **Every window opens after an update**: Updating or reloading AcesMotion while Studio was open could leave some windows unbuilt, starting with the Inspector. Each window now replaces any leftover copy from before the update.
  • **Viewport picks stay on what you're picking**: Picking a Socket, Parent to or Attached to part in the viewport no longer selects that part in Studio and moves the Inspector away. A chip in the viewport says what you're picking, and pressing the pick button again cancels.
  • **Space plays from the viewport**: Space plays and pauses with the viewport, Hub or the Inspector focused, not only the Timeline. It does nothing while you type or with no scene open.

0.15.3 2026-10-28

Fixed

  • **Joints no longer vanish between keys**: In 0.15.2, two keys with almost the same rotation could break a joint's rotation between them, and the part disappeared. The joint now stays put for that tiny turn, and no key is ever saved with a broken value.

0.15.2 2026-10-28

New

  • **Pick several things and change them all at once**: Ctrl+click or Shift+click layers, versions, scenes, markers or actors, or Alt+click pose cards. Delete, duplicate, mute, recolour or move them in one undo step. Shift+drag along the ruler to box select markers.
  • **Picked parts count as selected rows**: Joints you pick in the viewport take Add constraint, Motion Warp, generator and driver just like their rows, and the viewport's right-click menu has an Add submenu. When a target is needed, you pick it once for all of them.
  • **Pose tools work on several actors**: Paste pose, Paste mirrored, Flip pose and Reset to rest act on every picked actor at once.
  • **Between keys: Shortest turn or Each angle on its own**: Set how a rotation track goes from key to key in the row menu, the key menu's Rotation submenu or the Inspector's Key panel. With several rows selected, the row menu sets them all in one undo step.
  • **A clearer changelog and What's new**: Settings > About > Changelog lists every version with a search, New, Improved and Fixed filters and a card per release. What's new covers everything since the version you last saw.

Improved

  • **X switches between IK and FK**: Press X in the viewport to switch. It stays switched until you press X again, instead of only while you hold I. Mid drag, X still locks the X axis, and a key you rebound before keeps working.

Fixed

  • **Torsos and limbs no longer lift between keys**: When a joint's X, Y and Z keys sit on the same frames, it takes the shortest turn between them, the way Roblox's Animation Editor blends poses. Eases and overshoot still shape it. Camera roots keep each angle on its own curve, so a camera never rolls.
  • **Windows stay where you put them**: Docked and floating windows keep their place and size when AcesMotion updates or reloads.

0.15.1 2026-10-28

New

  • **Keyed AcesFX settings move their followers**: Keying a setting others follow, such as a Ride time a Duration follows, moves its followers with the link's Scale and Offset while you scrub, play and in games.
  • **Linked rows show what they follow**: A follower can still be keyed, and its own keys win. Its row shows a link icon, and its tooltips say what it follows.

0.15.0 2026-10-25

New

  • **AcesFX blocks: Emit, Enable, Disable, Enabled**: Play an actor's AcesFX effects right where they are. Add them from Add block > AcesFX; each targets the whole actor or one part.
  • **Scrub AcesFX effects frame by frame**: Bursts, streams, riders, lights, beams, lightning, trails, sounds and camera shake show as they play at the frame you're on, forward or back.
  • **Timing tails behind AcesFX blocks**: Ticks for bursts, a stripe while a part emits, the held band, the wind-down and an end cap. Tail ends and bursts are snap targets.
  • **AcesFX blocks in the Inspector**: Target, Scale, lightning targets, what it fires and for how long, and Select in AcesFX.
  • **AcesFX blocks play in games**: Each client plays them through the game's AcesFX runtime, following jumps, loops, SetSpeed and Pause. Effects = false skips them.
  • **Trails while scrubbing**: On by default. Every Trail draws from where its attachments were while you scrub, pause or play faster than 1x.
  • **Hide empty rows in pickers**: A filter button beside the search hides greyed out rows. Also in the list's right-click menu and Settings > Look.

Improved

  • **Lighter scrubbing with thousands of particles**: The preview simulates only the particles its cap shows, and rebuilds a moving particle's frozen copy far less often.

0.14.3 2026-10-23

New

  • **Esc or an empty click selects nothing**: Clears picked joints, keys, blocks, rows and actors, from the viewport, the lanes or the space under the last row.

Fixed

  • **Row clicks and viewport picks share one selection**: Clicking a joint's row lets go of the joints picked in the viewport, and the other way round, so old picks no longer stay highlighted.
  • **Row buttons act on viewport picks**: Stack, Mute, Solo and Lock act on every selected row when the joints are picked in the viewport.
  • **Stack rows switches every row again**: A row set back by its own stack button follows Stack rows, and switching Stack rows clears the rows' own choices.

0.14.2 2026-10-23

New

  • **Hide while playing**: On by default, in the View menu and Settings > Gizmos: while the scene plays, the gizmo, joint dots, bones, highlights, the trail, guides and camera frustums step aside so you watch the animation alone.
  • **Stack rows**: A button on the rows header, View, the rows filter menu, Settings > Timeline; each row also has its own stack button beside Mute, to hide one row while it's on or show one row while it's off. Turn it off to hide the generator, constraint and driver lanes under their rows, so you animate keys alone and a box select takes keys only. The blocks keep playing.
  • **Add constraints and drivers to several rows**: Select several rows and right-click one: Add constraint and Add driver put the pick on every selected row in one undo step, as Add generator already did, with its target picked once for all of them.

Fixed

  • **Duplicated stack blocks land below the original**: Duplicating a block on a stack row (a generator, constraint or driver) puts the copy in the sub-lane below at the same time, instead of after the scene's end where it couldn't be seen.
  • **Stack rows fit the lanes their blocks use**: A stack row is as tall as the sub-lanes its blocks use: a lone block moved to a lower lane no longer leaves empty lanes above it.
  • **IK on custom R6 rigs bends the limb you drag**: IK posing on a custom R6 rig bends the arm or leg you drag instead of swinging the whole body: limbs named without the space (LeftArm, RightLeg) were taken for held props, and moved their holder, the torso. An IK chain set longer than the limb never takes the rig's root joint either.
  • **Locks hold R6 feet and hands**: A limb that is one joint turns at the hip or shoulder to keep its end on the spot, instead of keeping its angle and sliding with the body.
  • **Locked feet stay planted at a lock's first frame**: A lock with no Spot yet gets the spot it holds before the pose moves anything, so posing the hips no longer drags it along.

0.14.1 2026-10-23

New

  • **Scenes play on rigs in a ViewportFrame**: Such as an inventory card or a reveal. A card is judged by how it shows on screen instead of by the world camera: it rests while hidden, disabled or scrolled out of its list, MaxDistance leaves it alone, and copies the scene spawns stay inside the card.

Improved

  • **Custom modules keep their id under their own attribute**: Custom generators, constraints and drivers use AcesMotion_GeneratorId, AcesMotion_ConstraintId and AcesMotion_DriverId. Modules made earlier keep working; their AcesMotion_ActionId is still read and copied over the next time the plugin touches them.
  • **A custom action's Scrub error warns in games**: A custom action whose Scrub errors in a game now warns once in Output, as it does in Studio.
  • **Custom constraint errors warn once**: A custom constraint whose Solve errors or returns no CFrame warns once in Output, like generators and drivers.
  • **Cast accepts typed lists under strict mode**: AcesMotion.Cast accepts a typed list such as { Model } for a list role under --!strict.

Fixed

  • **NPCs end where players saw them after PlayForAll**: After a PlayForAll, the server puts an NPC where players saw it end, Motion Warp and attach keys included, and a prop the scene carries and drops lands where it was dropped instead of staying behind on the server.
  • **Camera scenes ignore MaxDistance**: With MaxDistance set, a scene that drives the camera keeps playing however far its anchor is, instead of freezing the view.
  • **Ease window handles update as you drag**: Dragging a handle in the Ease window updates the curve, the fields and the scene as you drag, not only when you let go.
  • **Zoomed-out curves no longer draw flat**: Curves no longer draw flat on their zero line when the curve editor is zoomed out to a wide range of values (0 to 100 degrees in a short graph).
  • **Destroying a Track disconnects every signal**: Destroying a Track also disconnects LengthChanged, CameraAttached, CameraDetached and ShotChanged.
  • **Custom drivers with no target change nothing**: A custom driver with a required target left unset changes nothing, like the built-in drivers, so the value under it plays.
  • **Custom generators can have their own Low, High, Speed**: A custom generator can have its own Low, High, Speed or StepRate setting: the Between Low and High, Scroll and Pattern modes keep their values apart from it.

0.14.0 2026-10-23

New

  • **Screen effects**: Add them from the + menu, the Cast tab or the Camera row, then key letterbox bars (top and bottom), a vignette, blur, a fade to any colour, colour correction and bloom. Studio shows them while you look through a camera, and games draw them on the player's screen while a scene has the camera, easing in and out with it.
  • **Depth of field per camera**: A Lens section with focus distance, Focus on (an actor or part the focus follows), in-focus radius and near and far blur.
  • **Hold / Fade generator and screen effect presets**: Hold / Fade, a new generator, and Screen effects presets in the Library: Fade to black, Fade from black, Flash white, Letterbox and Vignette pulse. The Screen row's menu adds fades, a flash and letterbox bars at the playhead.
  • **A fighter's point of view**: Attach a camera to a head from its Camera section and press Eyes. In games it follows the head as it really moves, the game's own animation included.
  • **Scriptable cameras for games**: Camera = "Manual" works the scene's camera out without touching the player's, read with GetCamera (its screen effects and lens too), with AttachCamera, DetachCamera and the CameraAttached, CameraDetached and ShotChanged signals.
  • **Camera in game settings**: Press the Camera row to set how a game's camera glides into the scene and back out, with its own ease, and what happens when the scene is stopped early.
  • **Reorder actors by dragging**: Drag them in the Cast tab or on the Timeline. A scene can keep its own Timeline order.
  • **OKLab and OKLCH colour blends**: For colour tracks and the gradient editor: colours fade from one to the next in even, natural steps.

Improved

  • **Cameras turn like a camera head**: The rotate gizmo pans and tilts without rolling, and pans and orbits blend smoothly between keys.
  • **Tooltips on menu items**: Menu items that don't explain themselves have tooltips.
  • **Peak sensitivity setting**: Settings > Audio > Peak sensitivity sets how quiet a waveform hit can be and still snap.
  • **The Sequence editor opens taller**

Fixed

  • **Cameras no longer tilt or roll between keys**: A camera in a scene that measures root motion from each actor's root part no longer tilts up or rolls between keys, and plays in Studio as it does in games.
  • **Posing a Lock's target moves the locked part**: Also at the lock's first frame.
  • **Reset to default on screen effects and lens**: Reset to default works on screen effect and lens values.

0.13.0 2026-10-22

New

  • **Scene versions**: Alt+Shift+S or the Hub's new History tab saves a version of the scene, and automatic ones are kept as you work, on open and close, before exports and before heavy edits. Preview one read-only, Compare it as a ghost with faint keys while you work, Restore the whole scene or only the selected actors, or Branch it into a new scene.
  • **A dot marks unsaved changes**: A dot after the scene name marks changes no version you saved holds, in the Timeline, the Hub and the scene lists.
  • **Toasts show what happened**: Toasts show what happened in the window you work in, with Undo and other actions where they help. Click the status bar's message for the recent ones; Settings > Look sets how long they stay and where.
  • **Save as names and opens the copy**: Save as asks for the copy's name and opens the copy.
  • **Pose library**: Save poses for any rig, apply them with a click or a slot key (Alt+1 to Alt+9), blend toward one with an Alt drag, and find one fast with the quick pose picker (Ctrl+Alt+P). Poses are yours and travel with Export presets.
  • **Locks plant feet and hold hands**: Plant a foot or hold a hand where it is, in the world or on another actor, while the rest of the body moves. Lock between selected keys, Shift+L on a picked part, or turn pins into locks; posing a locked part keys where the lock holds it, so one lock can run the whole timeline. Chain length, Spot only and a viewport marker that turns red when the limb can't reach. Locks play in games and export natively.
  • **Let the prop lead**: Key a held sword's swing and the arm follows it.

Improved

  • **Bars no longer add main keys**: Constraints, generators, drivers and action blocks no longer add main keys, so a main key moves only keys.
  • **Copy and paste is context aware**: A copied block pastes onto the selected block or row.
  • **Export and import presets through ServerStorage**: Export presets puts a folder in ServerStorage and selects it, and Import presets reads the export selected in the Explorer.
  • **Settings for automatic versions**: Settings > Versions sets automatic versions and how many this place keeps.

Fixed

  • **Keying on a layer keeps the pose you see**: Keying on a layer, or on Base under layers, keeps the pose you see instead of adding the layers above on top of it.
  • **Keys sit centred on the playhead**: Keys sit centred on the playhead line at every zoom and UI scale.
  • **Floor measures the pose at its frame**: A Floor on a rig measures its pose at that frame, keeps a locked foot on the floor instead of lifting the rig early, and Bounding box now counts each part's box.
  • **Blocks dragged past the end come back**: A block dragged past the scene's end can be dragged back.

0.12.0 2026-10-21

New

  • **Scene layers**: Animate on top of Base with Override or Additive layers, each with a keyable Weight, a mask, Mute and Solo. Manage them in the Hub's Layers tab and switch with the toolbar's layer picker; the Timeline shows one layer at a time.
  • **Posing on a layer saves what you see**: An Additive key holds the difference from the layers below, an Override key the blend that lands on your pose.
  • **Show other layers, Merge down, Bake to Base**: Show other layers draws the other layers' keys as faint dots for timing. Merge down and Bake to Base fold layers without changing the pose.
  • **Layers play in games**: Layers play in games as in Studio, and options.Layers or Track:SetLayerWeight fades a layer by name. Native export bakes the layers in.
  • **A Gizmos page in Settings**: It gathers every gizmo, bone and joint dot setting, with bone colour, thickness and transparency and joint dot colour, size and transparency.
  • **A colour picker on every colour setting**: Every colour setting has a colour picker: click its swatch.

Fixed

  • **Turns past 90 degrees keep their axis**: A joint turned past 90 degrees about one axis keeps that turn instead of flipping to a half turn on the other two, so rigs no longer roll between keys. Turns past 180 degrees and whole turns keep going the way they were turned, in keys, bakes, merges, imports and exports.

0.11.0 2026-10-21

New

  • **Held props ride a joint in games**: A part or model attached to a hand costs nothing per frame. Unanchor, Massless and Non-colliding switches in the Attached group say what it is while held, and attaching an anchored prop offers to unanchor it.
  • **Command palette**: Press F3 to find and run any command, with your recent ones first.
  • **Rebind every shortcut, search Settings**: Every shortcut can be rebound in Settings, and Settings has a search across all of its pages.
  • **Viewport right-click menu**: Right click in the viewport for a menu of posing, keying and attach commands.
  • **Tween slider**: Blend the picked joints between their keys into Breakdowns.
  • **Solo, and curves before and after their keys**: Solo rows and actors in the editor, and set what a curve does before and after its keys: Hold, Cycle, or Cycle with offset.
  • **Copy and paste blocks and switch keys**: Copy and paste blocks, Camera switch keys and attach keys. Presets drag from the Library onto Timeline rows.
  • **More ways to select keys**: Select keys before, after or at the playhead, by key type, or inverted. Selected keys show a Key panel in the Inspector.
  • **Typed amounts and axis locks on gizmo drags**: Gizmo drags take typed amounts, X, Y and Z axis locks and a Shift fine drag. An Arrow length setting, with shorter arrows by default.

Improved
