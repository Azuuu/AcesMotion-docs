Roadmap ​

AcesMotion is a work in progress, built in phases. Everything on this page is planned and not in the plugin yet.

Highlights ​

UI animationsPlanned

Animate ScreenGuis and SurfaceGuis on the same Timeline: menus, HUD reveals and cinematic titles.

Physics bakingPlanned

Simulate debris, ragdolls and props, then bake the result into keys you can edit.

ClipsPlanned

Save a range as a clip, place it on an actor, then trim, loop, speed it up and blend it in and out.

Rigging and IKPlanned

An IK builder with pole targets, IK to FK blending and match, root motion tools and dragging the trail through IK.

More constraintsPlanned

Look At Chain across chest, neck, head and eyes, Two-Shot framing, Orbit, Follow Path and live IK to a target; later Spring and screen composition.

Environment layerFuture

Key the sky, atmosphere, clouds and lighting on the Timeline, the same way as screen effects, plus subtitles.

RetargetingPlanned

Move animation between R6, R15 and custom rigs, and mirror a whole animation.

Everything planned ​

None of these exist in the plugin today. One line each.

Action tracks, camera and audio ​

  • (Emit, Enable, Spawn Effect, Camera Shake and Cut are not actions any more: Emit() keys, Bool tracks, Attach / Detach, generators, camera switch keys.)
  • The block panel's leftovers: a sound asset's length beside its name, the image mosaic for several blocks with different images, the live "+0.40" change while dragging a Mixed Start or Length.
  • The marker panel for several markers at once (multi-selection of markers on the ruler, Mixed fields and payload rows), the searchable part and joint popovers, and the viewport hover outline and name chip while picking a part.

After that ​

  • More constraints: Look At Chain (an aim spread over chest, neck, head and eyes), Stabilize, Between, Mirror, Two-Shot (keeps several targets framed), Orbit Target, Follow Path, and live IK to a target; later Spring and Frame (screen composition), with custom constraints that remember the frame before.
  • Further out: subtitles that work in Studio and in game, and an environment layer (sky, atmosphere, clouds and lighting) keyed like the screen effects.

Settings pages and Timeline settings ​

  • Settings > Navigation: presets (AcesMotion, Blender, Maya), zoom and pan speed, a 13-row bindings table with key capture.
  • Settings > Audio: peak sensitivity.

Windows and panels ​

  • Inspector states for a modifier, the scene and an actor's own settings (including the Mirror rules summary row).
  • The Library's Clips tab.
  • Team Create: a toast saying what a teammate changed ("Maya moved 12 keys on Hero"), a note for teammates after a whole-scene change, a ping that shows on your teammate's Timeline, and "everyone follow me" for reviews.
  • Update checks against GitHub releases as well as the store listing; About's Report an issue button.

Timeline ​

  • Joint hierarchy groups.
  • Action and sound blocks and scene audio lanes on the ruler.
  • Audio peak ticks on a sound lane.
  • Double click on a value bar to select its key.
  • An animated Loading spinner on sound bars.

Layers and clips ​

  • Per-joint mask weights and a Feather fill for layer masks; baking modifiers in export.
  • Clips: save a range, place it on an actor, trim, loop, speed, blend.
  • Noise modifier and the remaining generators (Bounce, Anticipation, Follow-through offset, Exaggerate, Moving hold, Loop cleaner, Simplify/Bake, Reverse/Mirror, Randomize, Camera presets).
  • Live curve modifiers: Noise, Cycles, Stepped, Envelope, Limits, Smooth (baked on native export).

Rigging and IK ​

  • IK builder: chain, pole target, IK to FK blend and match (Settings > Posing > Pole behaviour "Pole target").
  • Rig prop to root, and bake-to-Motor6D export for attached props.
  • Root motion extract and bake into the hips.
  • Trajectory dragging through IK chains.
  • Holding hips, shoulders, wrists and ankles to their constraint limits, and a placed pole target left on a limb.

Power tools ​

  • Retargeting between R6, R15 and custom rigs (a bone map editor).
  • Mirror a whole animation.
  • Overlap and follow-through modifier.
  • Weighted tangents (resampled on native export).

Smaller items ​

  • Virtualised Inspector rows (every row of an open section exists today).
